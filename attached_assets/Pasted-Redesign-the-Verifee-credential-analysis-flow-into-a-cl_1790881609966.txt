Redesign the Verifee credential-analysis flow into a clean four-step wizard/stepper.

Do not change the existing visual identity dramatically. Keep the current high-trust, minimal, university/fintech-like aesthetic, typography, muted green palette, whitespace, borders and professional feel.

### Replace the current layout

Remove the large right-side "How Verifee Works" explanatory panel.

Instead, create a horizontal stepper across the top of the application:

1. Upload Credential
2. Verify & Review
3. Select Target
4. Academic Mapping

Only the current step should be visually emphasized.

### STEP 1 — Upload Credential

Heading:

**Upload an academic credential**

Supporting text:

"Upload an English-language academic record. Verifee can interpret standard transcripts and can verify supported digital credential formats."

Create one large centered upload/drop zone.

Clearly show the three accepted formats:

**PDF**
Standard transcript or Diploma Supplement

**OpenCerts (.opencert)**
Digitally verifiable credential

**European Digital Credential (.jsonld)**
Machine-readable European credential

Drop-zone copy:

**Drop your credential here or browse files**

Below:

`PDF, .opencert, or .jsonld · 20 MB maximum`

Add a subtle informational note:

"Supported digital credentials are checked for authenticity. Standard PDFs can still be interpreted and mapped but may require external verification."

Do not show the Georgia Tech target-selection dropdown on this step.

### STEP 2 — Verify & Review

After upload, show a structured credential summary.

Sections:

**Credential format**
Example: OpenCerts / European Digital Credential / PDF transcript

**Verification status**
Possible states:
- Digitally verified
- Verification unavailable
- Verification failed
- External verification recommended

**Issuer**

**Qualification**

**Field of study**

**Country / education system**

**Courses detected**

Clearly separate credential verification from academic interpretation.

### STEP 4 — Academic Mapping

Retain and improve the existing mapping results.

Use the four states:

- Covered
- Partially covered
- Potential gap
- Insufficient evidence

For every result show:
- target requirement
- matching student course(s)
- evidence
- concise rationale
- High / Medium / Low confidence

Also show an overall credential verification status at the top of the report.

Keep this disclaimer:

"Verifee provides preliminary academic interpretation and mapping. It is not an official admissions, credential-equivalency, or transfer-credit determination."
