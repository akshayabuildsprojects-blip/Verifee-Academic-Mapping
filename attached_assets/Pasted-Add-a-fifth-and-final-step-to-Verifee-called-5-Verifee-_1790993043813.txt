Add a fifth and final step to Verifee called:

5. Verifee Report

Do not change the existing credential extraction, academic mapping, evidence retrieval, or Step 4 results.

The purpose of Step 5 is to convert the completed Verifee analysis into a professional downloadable PDF report.

The report should use the SAME completed mapping data already stored in application state. Do not rerun AI analysis when generating the report.
