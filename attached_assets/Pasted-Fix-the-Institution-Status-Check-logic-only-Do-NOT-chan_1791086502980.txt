Fix the Institution Status Check logic only.

Do NOT change transcript extraction, academic mapping, target-program logic, report generation, or other workflow behavior.

PROBLEM

The current status check requires the transcript itself to explicitly contain a jurisdiction before selecting an institution registry.

This causes valid institutions such as the University of Strathclyde to return:

"Unable to check"

because the transcript does not literally state the jurisdiction.

That is too restrictive.

--------------------------------------------------
NEW INSTITUTION RESOLUTION FLOW
--------------------------------------------------

Before selecting a registry:

1. Read the institution name extracted from the transcript.

2. Attempt to resolve:
   - canonical institution name
   - country
   - subnational jurisdiction where relevant

3. Use authoritative evidence to resolve jurisdiction where possible.

Examples:

University of Strathclyde
→ Scotland
→ United Kingdom

Nanyang Technological University
→ Singapore

Georgia Institute of Technology
→ Georgia
→ United States

Do not require the jurisdiction to be literally stated in the transcript.

--------------------------------------------------
SOURCE LABELING
--------------------------------------------------

Clearly distinguish:

Source: Transcript

from:

Resolved by Verifee

Example:

Institution:
University of Strathclyde
Source: Transcript

Jurisdiction:
Scotland, United Kingdom
Source: Resolved by Verifee

--------------------------------------------------
REGISTRY SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------

After jurisdiction resolution, select the appropriate authoritative source.

Do NOT treat the United Kingdom as one registry jurisdiction.

At minimum distinguish:

England
→ Office for Students where appropriate

Scotland
→ Scottish/UK authoritative recognised-institution source

Do not send Scottish universities to the Office for Students registry.

If no supported authoritative source exists for the resolved jurisdiction, show:

Institution identified
Jurisdiction resolved
Status check unavailable for this jurisdiction

Do not show:
"Jurisdiction not stated in transcript"

if Verifee has successfully resolved it.

--------------------------------------------------
STATUS TERMINOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------

Do not use the generic term "accredited" unless the authoritative source itself establishes accreditation.

Prefer source-specific language such as:

Listed
Recognised
Registered
Found in official institution registry

Show the exact source used.

--------------------------------------------------
NO GUESSING
--------------------------------------------------

If institution identity is ambiguous, do not guess.

Return:

Institution identity requires review

If jurisdiction cannot be resolved confidently, return:

Jurisdiction unresolved

Only perform the registry check after the institution and jurisdiction have been resolved with sufficient confidence.
