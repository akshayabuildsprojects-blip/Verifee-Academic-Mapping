STAGE C — FINAL ACADEMIC REASONING
--------------------------------------------------

After obtaining evidence for the shortlisted courses, compare that evidence against the Georgia Tech requirement.

PARTIALLY_COVERED

INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
