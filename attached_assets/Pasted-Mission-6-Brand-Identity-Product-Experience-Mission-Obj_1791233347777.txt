Mission 6: Brand Identity & Product Experience
Mission Objective: Make the Product Clear, Trustworthy, and Memorable
Give the working product a recognizable identity inside the experience customers actually use, then begin the pitch deck that tells the same truthful story. Keep the original brand identity, visual direction, and short story work, but let customer friction determine which part of the product to improve first. The “cool factor” is a useful product moment that helps a person understand, act, or see a meaningful result—not decoration over a broken task.

⚡ AI Tools & Tactics for Acceleration
🗣 Verbal Identity (Story + Tone)
ChatGPT: Draft brand personality words, tone of voice, and your 2–3 sentence story.
Prompt: “Act as a brand strategist. Suggest 5 brand personality adjectives and write a 3-sentence brand story for a [venture idea] targeting [audience].” - This is where you can enter your content & context about your company, i.e. Mission 2 & 3!
🎨 Visual Identity (Colors, Fonts, Moodboard)
Coolors.co / Khroma: Generate color palettes from keywords.
Looka / Hatchful by Shopify: Fast AI logo drafts to spark direction.
Canva AI / Adobe Firefly: Create moodboards or mock visuals from text prompts.
Prompt: “Generate a moodboard that captures a brand identity that is [adjective 1], [adjective 2], and [adjective 3]. Include colors and design elements.”
🧪 Rapid Testing
UsabilityHub / Maze: Quick A/B testing of logos, colors, or taglines with real users.
MidJourney / DALL·E: Create concept art or mock marketing visuals to see how your brand “shows up” in the world.
Pro Tips:
Anchor in Emotion: If your persona feels “stressed,” your brand should feel “relieving.”
Design for Versatility: Pick colors and fonts that work on app screens, decks, and social media.
One-Liner Test: If you had 5 seconds on stage, could your brand story line land clearly?
Consistency is King: Every element (logo, copy, visuals) should feel like they belong in the same family.
Example:
Customers hesitate because they cannot tell whether the AI result is final. The team labels it “AI draft,” explains it plainly, and adds visible edit and approve controls using the chosen brand voice and visual system. A permitted customer or first-time tester attempts the revised task. The team reports what happened and uses that before/after interaction in its deck draft, without claiming a universal improvement from one attempt.
